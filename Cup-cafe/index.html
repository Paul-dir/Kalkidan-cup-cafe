COMMON CUSTOMER QUESTIONS

What types of coffee do you serve?

We offer a variety of Ethiopian coffee blends, including espresso, cappuccino, cold brew, and traditional buna prepared with a jebena. Our beans are sourced from top regions like Yirgacheffe and Sidamo, ensuring rich flavors.

Do you offer delivery services?

Yes, we provide fast and reliable delivery services across Addis Ababa for both individual orders and office catering. Order online or call us to enjoy our coffee at your convenience.

Are your pastries freshly made?

Absolutely! Our pastries are baked fresh daily, using high-quality ingredients. From croissants to traditional Ethiopian sweets, we ensure every bite is delicious.
